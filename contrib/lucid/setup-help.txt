SPOTIFAST LUCID

Start Spotifast Lucid from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

Follow the app's sign-in instructions to create your own Spotify developer
app and enter its Client ID. Spotify Premium is required.

Choose Set up Soloist, get your personal key from the linked Spotify page,
then Download and enable Soloist. Once, open official Spotify on the same
account/network and select the exact Spotifast Soloist device shown in setup.
If it is missing, fully quit and reopen official Spotify to refresh discovery.
Return to Spotifast and choose Start listening. Official Spotify can now close.

Themes: Settings > Appearance > Theme.
TrueVision is gold Raphael. GreatSage is the separate blue variant.

To update, fully quit Spotifast with Ctrl+Q, then run the newer installer.
Settings, keys, Soloist pairing and customised palettes are retained.
Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps. Your profile is kept for reinstall.
Do not send your configured app folder or keys to friends; send the installer.
